Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-26 · Read together with the Terms of Use.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, GetLearny processes your name, email address, date of birth, account and sign-in details, profile picture, class level, selected subjects, and parent or guardian approval records. We also process the study questions, chat messages, journal entries, test results, uploaded files, extracted study text, and learning activity you choose to save.

2. Verification and sign-in

Email sign-up uses a one-time verification code. We store a protected representation of the code and temporary registration details until verification or expiry. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides identity information such as your email address, display name and profile image so we can authenticate your account.

3. Parent or guardian approval

For accounts belonging to learners under 18, we collect a parent or guardian email address and store the approval status and related record. Approval links are time-limited and can only be used once. Some optional features may require separate guardian approval.

4. Study materials and AI

Text extracted from your uploaded study materials, along with the questions and relevant learning context you submit, may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response. Files may be stored with our configured file-storage provider. Do not upload sensitive personal information that is not needed for study.

5. Study Buddy, referrals and notifications

If you use Study Buddy or referral features, we process referral codes, invitation activity and related learning-streak summaries to create invite links and show participation counts. If you enable device notifications, your browser or device handles the permission; when background push is configured and enabled, we may store a push subscription and notification preferences. Reminder settings may include a selected time and device time-zone identifier.

6. Location

GetLearny does not currently collect, store, or use your device location or GPS coordinates. The location-consent setting records your choice only; it does not activate location tracking or sharing. If a location feature is introduced, we will update this notice and the Terms of Use before enabling it, explain what location data is used and why, and request separate explicit consent. Guardian approval will also be required where applicable.

7. How information is used and shared

We use information to operate accounts, provide tutoring and study tools, save progress, support invitations, deliver enabled notifications, maintain security, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations. Relevant information is shared only with service providers needed to operate the features you request, such as database, file-storage, email, authentication and AI providers. Their processing is subject to their own terms and privacy notices.

8. Security and retention

We use account controls and technical safeguards intended to protect information. Verification codes expire and are limited to a small number of attempts. No online service can guarantee perfect security. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account records, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes, then delete or de-identify it where appropriate.

9. Your choices and contact

You can choose whether to use optional features, disable notifications through your device or browser settings, and stop using the service. You or your parent or guardian can contact GetLearny support to ask about account information, privacy or withdrawal of a recorded consent through the supported account process.
