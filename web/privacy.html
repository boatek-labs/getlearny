Privacy Notice

Version 2026-11-26 · Read together with the Terms of Use.

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, GetLearny processes your name, email address, password hash, date of birth, class level, subjects, uploaded study materials, tutor messages, learning journal entries, lesson and practice activity, test results, notification settings and account preferences.

2. Verification and parent or guardian approval

Email verification codes are stored as hashes with an expiry time and attempt limit. For accounts belonging to learners under 18, we process the guardian email address and an approval record. Approval links contain random, time-limited tokens; the database stores a token hash rather than the original link token. A guardian decision is recorded and an approval link can only be used once.

3. Study buddies and referrals

If you use a study-buddy invitation or referral link, GetLearny may process the referral code and a record connecting the inviting account with the account created through that invitation. This supports invitation and referral counts. Sharing an invitation is optional; do not include private information in invitation messages.

4. Study materials and AI providers

Files you upload may be stored with the configured file-storage provider. When you ask the tutor for help, relevant text from your question, conversation, study materials or learning history may be sent to the AI provider configured for GetLearny so it can generate a response. Email providers deliver verification and parent or guardian messages. Hosting, database, storage and delivery providers process information as needed to operate the service. Do not upload sensitive personal information that is not needed for study.

5. Location

This version does not request device location permission and does not collect GPS coordinates or precise location data. A separate consent-preference record may be stored if location consent settings are used, including the choice, source, agreement version and relevant timestamps. That record does not enable location collection or sharing. If location features are introduced, this notice will be updated before they are made available and the required consent will be requested.

6. Security and retention

We use safeguards such as password hashing, time-limited verification tokens, access controls and protected service connections. No online service can guarantee perfect security. Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account and security records, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes, then deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

7. Your choices and contact

You can choose whether to use optional study-buddy invitations and notification features. You may stop using the service and contact GetLearny through the support contact published in the service to ask about your account or privacy. Parent or guardian approval and any supported consent choice can be changed through the account process. We will explain if a request cannot be completed.
