Privacy Notice

Version 2026-10-27 · Read together with the Terms of Use.

1. Information used

GetLearny processes account details, date of birth, study subjects, uploaded materials, learning activity and progress to provide its learning features and protect accounts.

2. Parent or guardian approval

For accounts belonging to learners under 18, we store a guardian email address and an approval record. Approval links are time-limited and can only be used once.

3. Study materials and AI

Text from materials and questions you submit may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response. Do not upload sensitive personal information that is not needed for study.

4. Location

This version does not collect location data. Consent records are separate from study progress and do not themselves enable location collection.

5. Security and retention

We use account controls and technical safeguards intended to protect data. No online service can guarantee perfect security. Data is retained as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

6. Choices and contact

You can stop using the service and contact GetLearny support to ask about your account or privacy. Parent or guardian approval can be withdrawn through the supported account process.
